# Bring Me

A multiplayer Roblox scavenger hunt party game where players race to find a requested item and return it to the central hub while tagging or evading rivals.

## Language

**Target Item**:
The single randomized object requested for the active round that players must locate and retrieve.
_Avoid_: Quest item, objective, relic

**Hub**:
The central staging area containing the round prompt screen and the Deposit Zone.
_Avoid_: Lobby, main room, base

**Deposit Zone**:
The designated area within the Hub that scores and consumes the Target Item upon carrier entry.
_Avoid_: Goal, drop box, altar

**Station**:
A distinct themed zone in the venue where items spawn and station doors lead.
_Avoid_: Room, map, section

**Carrier**:
The player currently holding the Target Item.
_Avoid_: Holder, possessor, runner

**Tag**:
A melee action against a Carrier that forces them to drop the Target Item onto the floor with a pickup debounce.
_Avoid_: Hit, tackle, slap, steal

**Safe Zone**:
The region of the Hub where Tagging is disabled to prevent deposit camping.
_Avoid_: Sanctuary, green zone, peace area

**Power-Up**:
A single-use consumable utility purchased with Soft Currency and activated during rounds.
_Avoid_: Perk, buff, ability, spell

**Speed Boost**:
A temporary movement speed increase that is suppressed while acting as Carrier.
_Avoid_: Sprint, haste, dash

**Item Radar**:
A temporary visual directional beacon pointing toward the active Target Item.
_Avoid_: Compass, tracker, wallhack, reveal

**Whiff Cooldown**:
The 1.0-second recovery penalty applied after an unsuccessful Tag attempt.
_Avoid_: Miss delay, swing cooldown, attack timer

**Soft Currency**:
Coins earned through participation and winning rounds, spent on consumable Power-Ups.
_Avoid_: Gold, cash, credits, gems

**Intermission**:
The 15-second staging window between rounds where station doors are locked and players prepare.
_Avoid_: Lobby time, downtime, pre-game

**Overhead Weld**:
The physical attachment constraint pinning the Target Item above the Carrier's avatar for global visibility.
_Avoid_: Hand tool, pocketed item, equipped gear

**Highlight**:
A luminous boundary outline rendered around the Carrier to ensure high visual readability across the arena.
_Avoid_: Aura, glow effect, beacon

**Leaderboard**:
The dual scoring surface consisting of tab-list session `leaderstats` and an in-world all-time `OrderedDataStore` billboard.
_Avoid_: Scoreboard, ranking table, high scores
